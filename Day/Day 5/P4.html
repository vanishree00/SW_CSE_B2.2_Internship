Viewport: visible area of a webpage. It is the rectangle region where the browser actually renders and show content. Anything outside the vieport is not visible until we scroll.

CSS Units: the measurement units used to specify sizes, spacing and other length related values

1. Absolute unit: fixed values: px(pixels),cm,pt(points),pc(picas) 2. Relative unit: relative to something else : em (relates to parent element) | rem (root em) | % percentage | vw (viewport width) | vh (viewport height) | vmin: 1% of the smaller dimension of viewport | vmax: 1% of the larger dimension of viewport | zero value: margin: 0
